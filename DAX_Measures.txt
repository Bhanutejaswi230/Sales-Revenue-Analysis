DAX MEASURES FOR SALES & REVENUE ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

Assume the table name is: Sales_Data

1. Total Revenue
Total Revenue = SUM(Sales_Data[Revenue])

2. Total Profit
Total Profit = SUM(Sales_Data[Profit])

3. Total Orders
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales_Data[Order_ID])

4. Total Quantity
Total Quantity = SUM(Sales_Data[Quantity])

5. Profit Margin
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)

6. Average Order Value
Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Orders], 0)

7. Revenue Previous Month
Revenue Previous Month =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    DATEADD(Sales_Data[Order_Date], -1, MONTH)
)

8. Revenue Growth %
Revenue Growth % =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue] - [Revenue Previous Month],
    [Revenue Previous Month],
    0
)
